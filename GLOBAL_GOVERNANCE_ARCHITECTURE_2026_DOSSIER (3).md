- Gibraltar
- Montserrat
- St Helena

### TERRITORIES FAILING COMMITMENTS
- British Virgin Islands: Tipping-off provisions alert criminals under investigation
- Bermuda: Not expecting delivery until 2026
- Cayman Islands: High barriers to access; case-by-case applications
- Crown Dependencies (Jersey, Guernsey, Isle of Man): Committed to "legitimate interest" registers (EU-style), but none operational or publicly accessible despite 7+ years of promises

### KEY CONCERNS
- Narrow interpretation of European "legitimate interest" model
- Refusal to follow EU guidance
- Incomplete data hiding beneficial owners behind trust structures
- Restricted search functionality requiring prior knowledge of company names
- Years of delays in operationalizing registers

### CORRUPTION CASES LINKED TO OFFSHORE SECRECY
- 1MDB scandal
- PrivatBank theft
- Glencore Paradise Papers revelations

### LEGITIMATE INTEREST REGISTER MODEL
- Limits ability of journalists, civil society, and law enforcement to combat money laundering
- Jurisdictions unlikely to score better than "F" grade for access to beneficial ownership data
- Only Gibraltar, Montserrat, and St Helena established fully public registers
