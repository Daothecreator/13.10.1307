## Facet: Baden-Baden Meetings & Conference Ecosystem

**Research Agent:** Facet_05_Baden_Baden_Analyst  
**Date:** 2026-06-12  
**Scope:** Baden-Baden (Germany) meetings, conferences, reinsurance ecosystem, media companies, automotive events, and political/diplomatic gatherings since 2000.  
**Sources:** 15 independent web searches; [^1] through [^35].

---

### Key Findings

- **Baden-Baden Reinsurance Meeting** is one of the most important global conventions for reinsurance and insurance industry experts, held annually since 1979 (originally the "German Marine Conference" until 1978). It attracts approximately 2,000–3,000 specialists from over 70 countries for face-to-face contract renewal discussions and risk assessments ahead of the January main renewal period. [^1][^2][^3]
- The 2024 meeting took place **October 20–24, 2024**; the 2025 meeting was held **October 19–23, 2025** (47th annual); and the 2026 meeting is already scheduled. [^1][^2][^4]
- The event is **decentralised** across venues in Baden-Baden city centre, including the **Kongresshaus**, **Kurhaus complex**, **Trinkhalle**, hotels, restaurants, and cafés. [^1][^2]
- **Guy Carpenter** hosts a prominent Reinsurance Symposium at the start of the Baden-Baden meeting each year, with senior executives from SCOR, Hannover Re, Zurich Insurance, Lloyd’s, and others speaking. [^5][^6]
- **Swiss Re** maintains a significant presence at Baden-Baden, holding annual media conferences and operating from the **Bad Hotel zum Hirsch** as its headquarters during the event. [^7][^8]
- **Major reinsurers** consistently attending include: Munich Re, Swiss Re, Hannover Re, SCOR, Lloyd’s, Guy Carpenter, Marsh, WTW (Willis Towers Watson), Beazley, and Moody's RMS, among others. [^3][^5][^9][^10]
- **Media Control** (media control GmbH / TMC Thomson Media Control GmbH & Co. KG) is a leading German market research and media monitoring company based in Baden-Baden, founded in 1992. It is the official chart investigator for entertainment sectors (books, music, etc.) and stifter (donor) of the **Deutsche Medienpreis** (German Media Prize). [^11][^12][^13]
- **ARTE Deutschland TV** is headquartered in Baden-Baden, as one of the three units of ARTE (the Franco-German cultural TV channel launched in 1991). ARTE GEIE is in Strasbourg, ARTE France in Paris, and ARTE Deutschland in Baden-Baden. It is non-commercial and funded primarily by TV licence fees. [^14][^15]
- **Südwestrundfunk (SWR)** operates a major broadcasting and production facility (SWR Funkhaus) in Baden-Baden, located idyllically at the Fremersberg. It produces numerous TV shows, radio programmes, and cultural events. [^16][^17]
- The **Kongresshaus Baden-Baden** offers capacity for up to **2,700 persons**, with 15 conference rooms, 2,400 m² foyer, and around 150 congresses held annually across medicine, business, and technology. Since 2021 it holds the DEKRA-certified hygiene seal. [^18][^19]
- The **Kurhaus Baden-Baden** provides eight historic function rooms for seminars, conferences, and exhibitions, and caters to gala dinners and receptions. [^20]
- The **International Oldtimer-Meeting Baden-Baden** is a major annual automotive/cultural event held since 1976 (49th edition in July 2026), taking place in the Kurpark and Kaiserallee, attracting over 20,000 visitors and 350+ classic cars. It is a Concours d’Élégance automobile. [^21][^22]
- The **Kurpark-Meeting** transforms the Kurgarten into a summer cultural venue with free concerts, gastronomy, and open-air events from late August to early September. [^23]
- The **Deutsche Medienpreis** (German Media Prize) is an annual glamorous gala ceremony held in Baden-Baden, sponsored by Media Control. Notable past recipients include **Nelson Mandela** (1999), **Barack Obama** (2016), **Gerhard Schröder** (2000), **Stefanie Graf & Andre Agassi** (2007), **Joachim Löw** (2014), **Ban Ki-moon**, and the **Dalai Lama**. [^24][^25][^26][^27]
- **Bilderberg Meeting** was held in Baden-Baden on **June 6–9, 1991**, at the Steigenberger Hotel Badischer Hof. The 39th Bilderberg Meeting had 110 participants from 18 European countries, the US, and Canada. Agenda items included: Eastern Europe economic prospects, Soviet Union developments, Middle East fallout, German economic reconciliation (Treuhand experience), Yugoslavia, and South Africa. [^28][^29][^30]
- **Extinction Rebellion and Debt4Climate activists** occupied the **Kongresshaus Baden-Baden** on **October 24, 2022**, during the reinsurance meeting, climbing the canopy with a banner reading *"Stop (re)insuring Climate"*. [^31][^32]
- **Climate activism** has targeted the reinsurance meeting repeatedly. The **Insure Our Future** campaign highlighted that Hannover Re, Swiss Re, and Munich Re adopted oil/gas exit policies, while others (SCOR, Lloyd’s) were criticised for insufficient action. [^32]
- **Daimler/Mercedes-Benz** is strongly associated with the broader Baden-Württemberg region. Factory tours and corporate events are available in Sindelfingen, Affalterbach (AMG), and Bremen, though no permanent Daimler factory is directly in Baden-Baden city itself. [^33][^34]
- **Baden-Baden** is a UNESCO World Heritage "Great Spa Towns of Europe" site, combining a 2,000-year spa tradition with modern conference infrastructure, and is marketed as Germany’s "smallest cosmopolitan city." [^18][^35]

### Major Players & Sources

- **Baden-Baden Reinsurance Meeting**: Annual global reinsurance convention; ~2,000–3,000 delegates; managed by Baden-Baden Convention & Visitors Office with industry host committee. [^1][^2]
- **Kongresshaus Baden-Baden**: Primary congress venue (2,700 capacity); DEKRA-certified hygiene seal since 2021. [^18][^19]
- **Kurhaus Baden-Baden**: Historic event venue with eight function rooms for conferences and galas. [^20]
- **Media Control**: Baden-Baden-based market research and media monitoring firm; official chart investigator; donor of Deutsche Medienpreis. [^11][^12][^13]
- **Südwestrundfunk (SWR)**: Public broadcaster with major Funkhaus (studio complex) in Baden-Baden; produces TV/radio and cultural events. [^16][^17]
- **ARTE Deutschland TV**: German member of the Franco-German ARTE cultural channel; headquarters in Baden-Baden. [^14][^15]
- **Swiss Re**: Major reinsurer with dedicated Baden-Baden media conference and event presence at Bad Hotel zum Hirsch. [^7][^8]
- **Guy Carpenter**: Reinsurance broker hosting the annual Baden-Baden Reinsurance Symposium. [^5][^6]
- **Hannover Re**: German reinsurance giant; CEO Clemens Jungsthöfel has spoken at Guy Carpenter symposia. [^5][^9]
- **Munich Re**: World’s largest reinsurer; regularly present at Baden-Baden meetings. [^32]
- **Moody’s Insurance Solutions / RMS**: Attend and hold meetings at Baden-Baden. [^10]
- **Vendelux**: Conference intelligence firm selling attendee lists for Baden-Baden Reinsurance Meeting. [^3]
- **International Oldtimer-Meeting Baden-Baden**: Annual classic car concours; organised by marmato agency; 20,000+ visitors. [^21][^22]
- **Baden-Baden Kur & Tourismus GmbH / Baden-Baden Tourismus GmbH**: City tourism and convention marketing body. [^18][^35]
- **Bilderberg Group**: Held 1991 meeting at Steigenberger Hotel Badischer Hof, Baden-Baden. [^28][^29]
- **Extinction Rebellion / Debt4Climate / Insure Our Future**: Climate activism groups targeting the reinsurance meeting. [^31][^32]
- **Daimler AG / Mercedes-Benz**: Automotive industry presence in region; factory tours in Sindelfingen; sponsorship ties to Deutsche Medienpreis (e.g., Dieter Zetsche as laureator). [^33][^34][^24]

### Trends & Signals

- **Reinsurance market hardening**: Baden-Baden meetings have increasingly focused on "hard market" conditions, alternative capital solutions (ILS), and rising demand for reinsurance due to climate and geopolitical uncertainty. [^5][^7][^8]
- **Cyber risk and renewable energy** are becoming dominant specialty topics at Baden-Baden, with Swiss Re and others highlighting cyber accumulation risks and the energy transition (>$200 billion in insurance premium by 2035). [^8][^10]
- **Climate activism escalation**: Direct protests at the 2022 reinsurance meeting signal increasing scrutiny of the reinsurance industry’s role in enabling fossil fuel extraction. [^31][^32]
- **Digital transformation**: The reinsurance industry discusses Industry 4.0 readiness, digital client interfaces, and AI-driven claims processing at Baden-Baden symposia. [^31][^9]
- **Media concentration**: Baden-Baden hosts three major media entities (Media Control, SWR, ARTE Deutschland), making it a significant regional media hub. [^18][^35]
- **High-profile political presence via Deutsche Medienpreis**: The annual gala brings global leaders (US presidents, UN Secretaries-General, Nobel laureates) to Baden-Baden, creating a nexus of media, politics, and corporate sponsorship. [^24][^25][^26]
- **Conference season clustering**: Baden-Baden is part of a global reinsurance conference season that begins with Monte Carlo Rendez-Vous (September) and continues to Baden-Baden (October), Singapore, and the US. [^9]
- **Post-COVID recovery**: The Kongresshaus implemented DEKRA hygiene standards and resumed full-capacity events by 2022. [^18]

---

### Controversies & Conflicting Claims

- **Reinsurance industry’s climate role**: Campaigners argue reinsurers are "society’s risk managers" yet continue to enable fossil fuel projects. Swiss Re, Hannover Re, and Munich Re adopted oil/gas exit policies by 2022, but SCOR and Lloyd’s were criticised for insufficient action. Reinsurers counter that they manage catastrophic risk and are transitioning portfolios. [^32]
- **Bilderberg 1991 secrecy**: The 1991 Bilderberg Meeting in Baden-Baden was held under strict confidentiality ("NOT FOR QUOTATION"). Agenda included sensitive geopolitical topics (Soviet collapse, Yugoslavia, Treuhand). Conspiracy theories about Bilderberg influence persist, though no verified evidence of illegal activity has emerged from the Baden-Baden conference. [^28][^29][^30]
- **Deutsche Medienpreis political optics**: Awarding prizes to serving or former politicians (e.g., Gerhard Schröder in 2001, Barack Obama in 2017) has drawn questions about whether the event serves as a platform for political networking under the guise of media recognition. [^24][^25]
- **Attendee list commercialisation**: Vendelux and similar firms sell enriched attendee lists with contact details, raising data privacy and commercial intelligence concerns around high-level financial gatherings. [^3]
- **Extinction Rebellion protest tactics**: The 2022 occupation of Kongresshaus was described as peaceful but disruptive; some industry participants framed it as an attack on legitimate business activity, while activists framed it as necessary civil disobedience. [^31][^32]

### Recommended Deep-Dive Areas

- **Deutsche Medienpreis attendee and sponsor lists**: Investigate the full roster of laureates, laudators, sponsors, and corporate tables across the years to identify recurring political and business networks operating under the media prize umbrella. [^24][^25][^26]
- **Bilderberg 1991 Baden-Baden participant list**: Cross-reference the 110 attendees (government, diplomacy, business, military, media) with subsequent careers, board appointments, and policy influence to assess long-term network effects. [^28][^29][^30]
- **Reinsurance meeting attendee intelligence**: Deep-dive into the commercial sale of attendee data (Vendelux, etc.) and the closed-door nature of bilateral meetings at hotels/restaurants outside the official Kongresshaus programme. [^3]
- **Media Control ownership and client base**: Media Control is a key node in German media analytics. Mapping its corporate ownership (TMC Thomson Media Control), major clients, and government contracts could reveal information-flow advantages. [^11][^12][^13]
- **SWR and ARTE Deutschland production records**: These broadcasters produce content in Baden-Baden. Investigating whether their programming or event coverage has intersected with reinsurance meeting participants or political figures could reveal media-influence channels. [^14][^15][^16][^17]
- **Kongresshaus and Kurhaus event calendars (2000–2024)**: A systematic review of all congresses, medical conferences, and corporate events held at these venues may reveal lesser-known summits or recurring closed meetings. [^18][^19][^20]
- **Oldtimer-Meeting and automotive industry ties**: The classic car event draws wealthy collectors and automotive executives. Cross-referencing participant lists with reinsurance or corporate attendees could reveal informal networking channels. [^21][^22]
- **Climate activism intelligence**: The Insure Our Future campaign and Extinction Rebellion have documented which reinsurers cover fossil fuels. This data could be cross-referenced with Baden-Baden meeting negotiations to map policy timelines. [^32]
- **Baden-Baden hospitality sector during reinsurance week**: Hotels such as the Brenners Park-Hotel & Spa and the Bad Hotel zum Hirsch are central to the decentralised meeting. Room blocks, private dining, and suite bookings may yield network insights. [^1][^8]
