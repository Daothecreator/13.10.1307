## 6. FINCEN FILES — 2024–2025 ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS

**Entity Class:** U.S. Department of the Treasury Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN)

### Established Facts
- **TD Bank — Record $1.3 Billion FinCEN Penalty (October 10, 2024):**
  - FinCEN assessed a **$1.3 billion civil money penalty** against TD Bank, N.A. and TD Bank USA, N.A. for violations of the Bank Secrecy Act (BSA). This is the **largest penalty against a depository institution in U.S. Treasury and FinCEN history**.
  - **DOJ Parallel Action:** TD Bank pleaded guilty to **conspiracy to commit money laundering** and BSA violations. Total penalties across all regulators exceeded **$3 billion** ($1.8 billion DOJ + $1.3 billion FinCEN + $450 million OCC civil money penalty).
  - **Transaction Monitoring Failure:** For nearly a decade, TD Bank failed to update its AML compliance program. The bank intentionally excluded domestic ACH transactions and check activity from monitoring systems, resulting in **92% of total transaction volume going unmonitored** between January 1, 2018, and April 12, 2024.
  - **Volume Unmonitored:** Approximately **$18.3 trillion** in customer transactions went unmonitored over a decade.
  - **SAR Failures:** TD Bank failed to file **thousands of Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs)** on transactions totaling approximately **$1.5 billion**.
  - **CTR Delays:** Delayed filing Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs) for large cash transactions.
  - **Criminal Proceeds Laundered:** Criminal networks laundered **over $670 million**, including proceeds from **drug trafficking, terrorist financing, and human trafficking**.
  - **Employee Bribery:** Bank employees accepted bribes to facilitate money laundering through processing suspicious cash deposits and opening accounts for shell companies.
  - **Remedial Measures:** Four-year independent monitorship imposed by FinCEN; three-year independent monitorship by DOJ; bank required to restructure U.S. compliance program.
- **Paxful — $3.5 Million Penalty (December 2025):**
  - FinCEN assessed a $3.5 million penalty against cryptocurrency platform Paxful for failing to register as a money services business (MSB), failing to maintain an AML program, and failing to file SARs.

**Entity Class:** Global economic statistics (World Inequality Lab / Paris School of Economics; UBS Global Wealth Report 2025)

### Established Facts
- **Global Wealth Distribution (World Inequality Report 2026, data through 2024/2025):**
  - **Top 10%** of global population owns **~75%** of total global wealth.
  - **Top 1%** controls **~37–40%** of global wealth.
  - **Bottom 50%** holds **~2%** of global wealth.
  - **Top 0.001%** (~56,000 adults worldwide) holds wealth share that grew from **~4% in 1995 to >6% in 2024/2025**.
  - **Top 0.001% owns three times more wealth than the bottom half of humanity combined** (~2.8 billion adults).
  - **Top one-in-100 million** (~56 adults worldwide) hold on average **$53 billion** each.
  - **Average wealth of a top 0.001% member:** nearly **$1 billion**.
  - **Wealth growth rate since 1990s:** Billionaire and centi-millionaire wealth grew at **~8% annually**, nearly double the growth rate of the bottom 50%.
- **Wealth Thresholds (to enter elite tiers):**
  - Leave bottom 50%: ≥$29,200 net worth
  - Enter top 10%: ≥$65,600 net worth
  - Enter top 0.001%: centi-millionaire status required
  - Enter top one-in-a-million: billionaire status required
- **UBS Global Wealth Report 2025:**
  - Global personal wealth grew 4.6% in 2024.
  - **~60 million millionaires** worldwide (~1.6% of global adult population).
  - **United States:** ~40% of global millionaires; 924 billionaires in 2025 (up from 835 in 2024).
  - U.S. now home to **31.7%** of global billionaire population.
- **U.S. Federal Reserve Distributional Financial Accounts (2024 release):**
  - **Top 1%:** ~33% of U.S. household wealth.
  - **Top 10%:** ~65–70% of U.S. household wealth.
  - **Bottom 50%:** ~2–3% of U.S. household wealth.
- **India (World Inequality Lab 2024):**
  - **Top 1%** holds **40.1%** of total household wealth.
  - **Top 0.001%** (billionaire class) wealth grew from **0.8% of national income (1991) to 14.5% (2022)**.
  - **Top 10 richest Indians** hold more wealth than **bottom 700 million Indians** (Oxfam India Inequality Report 2023).
  - Effective tax rate on wealthiest 167 individuals: ~26% (2017–2022), below marginal rates for middle-class salaried workers.
- **Pakistan (World Inequality Report 2026):**
  - **Top 10%** capture **42%** of national income; bottom 50% get **19%**.
  - **Top 10%** hold **59%** of total wealth; **top 1%** controls **24%**.
- **Sweden (outlier):** Income gap between top 10% and bottom 50% narrowed slightly (12→11 ratio, 2014–2024); wealth share of top 10% declined marginally (28.1%→27.2%). However, richest 1% still hold 27% of total assets.
