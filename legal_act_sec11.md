## PART VI: DIGITAL ASSET RE-REGULATION AND FINCEN ENFORCEMENT

### DIRECTIVE 11-012: MANDATORY REINSTATEMENT OF DIGITAL ASSET ENFORCEMENT

**FINDING OF VIOLATION:** The Blanche Memo of 7 April 2025, the SEC's dismissal of seven pending crypto registration cases, and the dissolution of the SEC FCPA Unit have created a regulatory vacuum in which digital asset markets operate without the transparency requirements imposed on traditional banking, without correspondent banking scrutiny, and without the criminal liability that attaches to unlicensed money transmission. This vacuum extends the sovereign immunity stack to blockchain infrastructure, enabling elite capital to migrate from physical institutions to digital ledgers beyond regulatory reach. FinCEN's record $1.3 billion penalty against TD Bank and $3.5 million penalty against Paxful demonstrate that enforcement against traditional and crypto financial institutions is possible and necessary.

**LEGAL AUTHORITY:** Bank Secrecy Act, 31 U.S.C. § 5318 et seq.; 18 U.S.C. § 1960 (prohibition of unlicensed money transmitting businesses); 31 U.S.C. § 5336 (Corporate Transparency Act); 15 U.S.C. § 78j(b) (securities fraud); 12 U.S.C. § 1818 (FDIC enforcement powers).

**MANDATORY REMEDY:**
1. IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the Blanche Memo of 7 April 2025 is hereby VACATED and DECLARED VOID. The Department of Justice shall, within 14 days, reinstate all pending digital asset enforcement actions dismissed pursuant to the Blanche Memo and open new investigations into:
   (a) All unlicensed money transmitting businesses operating in the United States with volume exceeding $1 million annually;
   (b) All cryptocurrency exchanges, custodians, and DeFi protocols that failed to file suspicious activity reports for the period 7 April 2025 through 6 June 2026;
   (c) All stablecoin issuers that failed to maintain 1:1 reserve transparency;
   (d) All cross-border digital asset transfers exceeding $10,000 that were not reported under 31 U.S.C. § 5313 (Currency Transaction Reports).
2. IT IS HEREBY DIRECTED that the SEC shall, within 30 days, reinstate all dismissed crypto registration cases and open new enforcement actions against:
   (a) All digital asset issuers that sold unregistered securities since 1 January 2020;
   (b) All crypto lending platforms that offered unregistered investment contracts;
   (c) All NFT marketplaces that facilitated wash trading or market manipulation.
3. IT IS HEREBY MANDATED that FinCEN shall, within 60 days, issue a special measure under 31 U.S.C. § 5318A against all mixing services, privacy coins, and non-custodial wallets that obfuscate transaction trails, requiring all U.S. financial institutions to deny correspondent account access to entities facilitating anonymous digital asset transfers.

**ENFORCEMENT MECHANISM:** The DOJ's failure to reinstate enforcement actions within 14 days shall result in the arrest of the Criminal Division Chief for contempt. The SEC's failure to reinstate cases within 30 days shall result in the arrest of the Enforcement Director for contempt. FinCEN's failure to issue the special measure within 60 days shall result in the arrest of the FinCEN Director for contempt. Any cryptocurrency exchange, mixing service, or privacy coin issuer that continues to operate in violation of reinstated requirements after the effective dates shall be subject to immediate asset seizure under 18 U.S.C. § 981 and criminal prosecution under 18 U.S.C. § 1960.
