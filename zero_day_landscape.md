## Search 4: DOJ/SEC/FTC Enforcement Actions 2025-2026

**Key Findings:**
- **DOJ Fraud Section 2025 Year in Review** (released Jan 2026):
  - Record-setting results: 265 defendants charged, $16B+ aggregate intended fraud loss, 25 trials in 17 districts, 15 corporate enforcement actions, ~$1B in global resolutions
  - Largest health care fraud takedown in DOJ history: $14.6B alleged intended losses
  - First corporate FCPA indictment in 15 years: voting machine company for bribery tied to 2016 Philippine elections
  - FCPA enforcement paused Feb 2025 by Trump EO; resumed H2 2025 with revised guidelines (June 2025) prioritizing cartel/TCO nexus, substantial bribes, national security
  - Three corporate FCPA actions: corporate indictment (voting machine company), DPA with South American telecom ($6M penalty), CEP declination for insurance carrier

- **SEC Enforcement FY 2025 / First Half FY 2026**:
  - SEC Chairman Paul Atkins pivoted away from "regulation through enforcement"
  - 60 cases in first half FY 2026; 48 (80%) included claims against at least one individual; 31 filed against only individuals
  - 7 insider trading cases (4 in life sciences); 6 market manipulation cases (pump-and-dump, ramp-and-dump, cross-border)
  - Cross-Border Task Force formed Sept 2025 targeting foreign-based frauds against US investors
  - Zero FCPA cases in first half FY 2026 (continuing decline from H2 FY 2025)
  - No off-channel communications cases (hallmark of prior administration)
  - SEC Enforcement Manual updated Feb 24, 2026 (first time since 2017)

- **2025 Enforcement Roundup / 2026 Expectations** (Cadwalader, Jan 5, 2026):
  - Slower start to 2025 due to FCPA pause and 43-day federal government shutdown
  - False Claims Act (FCA) took center stage in 2025; expected to continue in 2026
  - Expected uptick in FCPA enforcement in 2026 as Fraud Section moves past pause
  - Greater focus on individual prosecutions anticipated

- **FCPA Statistics (2015-2024)**:
  - Declining trend: 35 cases (2022) → 19 (2023) → 21 (2024) → 11 DOJ + 4 SEC (2024)
  - "FCPA-related" charges (wire/mail fraud, securities fraud, tax, money laundering) increasingly significant
